Government of Tamil Nadu - Department of Agriculture
PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi Yojana

Overview:
Under this scheme, all landholding farmers' families are provided with financial benefit of Rs. 6,000/- per annum in three equal instalments of Rs. 2000/- each.

Subsidies for Agricultural Machinery:
Under the Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM), subsidies are provided to farmers for purchasing various agricultural machinery and equipment.
- Tractors: Up to 50% subsidy for small, marginal, SC/ST, and women farmers. 40% for other farmers.
- Power Tillers: Up to 50% subsidy (max Rs. 85,000) for SC/ST, small, marginal, and women farmers. 40% (max Rs. 70,000) for others.
- Plant Protection Equipment (Sprayers): 50% subsidy for SC/ST, small, marginal, and women farmers. 40% for others.

Micro Irrigation Scheme (PMKSY):
100% subsidy is given for Small and Marginal farmers and 75% subsidy for other farmers for installing drip/sprinkler irrigation systems.
