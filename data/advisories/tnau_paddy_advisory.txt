Crop: Paddy (Rice)
Pest: Brown Plant Hopper (BPH)
Symptoms: Yellowing of leaves, hopper burn (drying of plants in circular patches).
Management: 
- Avoid excessive nitrogen application.
- Drain water from the field for 3-4 days during initial infestation.
- Apply Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) 5%.
- In severe cases, use Pymetrozine 50% WG at 120 g/acre or Dinotefuran 20% SG at 80 g/acre.

Disease: Rice Blast
Symptoms: Spindle-shaped lesions on leaves with grey centers and brown margins.
Management:
- Use resistant varieties like CO 51, ADT 53.
- Avoid late planting.
- Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP at 200 g/acre or Azoxystrobin 25% SC at 200 ml/acre.
